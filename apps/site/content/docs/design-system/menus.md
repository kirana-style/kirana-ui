# Menus

Two axes change every menu, the dropdown menu's, the select's and the others':

- **Menu colour.** `default` draws a menu in the page's colours; `inverted` draws it
  in the dark scheme on a light page; `default-translucent` and
  `inverted-translucent` make either one glass, a translucent fill over a blur.
- **Menu accent.** `subtle` highlights a row in the accent grey; `bold` highlights
  it in the primary colour.
